The reaction emoji itself is fixed by the request, so no computation there. But the message_id is selected via query and then written into the new message_reactions record as a reference, so it counts as a computation input under the "selected IDs supplying references" rule.

So computation_attributes is just messages.message_id, while written_attributes covers message_reactions.message_id and message_reactions.reaction_type, excluding user_id since that comes from authentication rather than being explicitly written.
