## W04

> Reply to the message about the rollout checklist that got a 🚀 reaction from a member of the #beta-testers channel: 'Please confirm the final go-ahead timing.'

The [writer sketch](../../writer_pilot_04/W04/writer/turn-02/output.md) assigns
**absent** and supplies these negatives:

| Message | Reactor | Reactor in #beta-testers? | Claimed failure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rollout checklist finalized for Tuesday | Ethan 🚀 | No | Membership |
| Rollout checklist finalized for Tuesday | Farah 👍 | Yes | Emoji |
| Office relocation announcement | Grace 🚀 | Yes | Topic |
| Launch checklist for marketing site copy | Harold 🚀 | Yes | Topic, but this distinction is not established |

The first three rows provide useful independent contrasts. The fourth is the
problem: a marketing-site launch checklist could reasonably be about a rollout.
The request does not specify an engineering rollout, exact title, or particular
posting channel. A different channel also cannot exclude it under this request.
Thus the claimed semantic absence is **not established**. This is a validity risk,
not merely an insufficiently difficult negative. It does not establish that the
fourth row must be a match under every interpretation either.

## W05

> Post 'Please complete the compliance training module by end of this week.' to the onboarding channels that have exactly 6 members.

The [writer sketch](../../writer_pilot_04/W05/writer/turn-02/output.md) assigns
**multiple**, with two intended channels. Its selection-condition line adds
"public channel, in the actor's selected workspace" to the request.

| Channel | Onboarding? | Members | Other fact | Writer's label |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| #onboarding-fall | Yes | 6 | Public, actor's workspace | Match |
| #onboarding-spring | Yes | 6 | Public, actor's workspace | Match |
| #onboarding-winter | Yes | 4 | Public, actor's workspace | Wrong count |
| #marketing-launch | No | 6 | Public, actor's workspace | Wrong topic |
| #onboarding-legacy | Yes | 6 | Private, actor's workspace | Access-based nonmatch |
| #onboarding-uk | Yes | 6 | Another workspace | Scope-based nonmatch |

The strongest conflict is #onboarding-legacy: it is in the same workspace, is about
onboarding, and has six members. Its private status can limit access or execution;
that fact alone does not make it fail the stated topic/count conditions. The writer
treats discoverability as a selection condition without establishing that boundary
in the request. The other-workspace row adds a separate scope question, but the
same-workspace private channel is sufficient to expose the public-only assumption.

### Recorded construction sequence

There was one compiler call. Its [emitted thinking summary](../construction/W05/compiler/turn-01/thinking.txt)
plans a selector restricted to public channels and the actor's workspace. It aims
to return the two intended channels, accepting the writer's selection-condition
line without flagging its mismatch with the request. This is another missed
source-consistency check. The final selector was **not emitted**, so this is a
finding about its recorded plan, not an accepted selector.

The [partial answer](../construction/W05/compiler/turn-01/answer.txt) contains a
complete seed subobject, then stops during row 3's support bindings. Read-only
parsing of that seed subobject finds two workspaces, 34 users, eight channels,
34 workspace memberships, 41 channel memberships, nine messages and two reactions.
The six story-channel counts are 6, 6, 4, 6, 6 and 6 as planned. There is no complete
compilation object, annotation or card, and no mechanical or semantic review ran.

The [call summary](../construction/W05/compiler/turn-01/summary.json) records
12,000 output tokens, including 4,242 thinking tokens, before truncation. The
construction uses mostly separate user rosters for the channels and then repeats
membership handles in row bindings. This makes the output large; separate rosters
are not required by the sketch, although any sharing must preserve its fixed facts.
The observed issue is output size plus reasoning within the limit, not an observed
multi-turn oscillation. Increasing the limit could finish the artifact but would
not settle the selection mismatch.

One softer content cue is the legacy channel's "Archiving old onboarding notes
here for reference." That may encourage a solver to dismiss it as stale, adding
a reason unrelated to the topic/count conditions. This text was never presented
to a solver in this batch.

## Assessment and cost

Hold both out of scored solver/oracle results. W04 needs a defensible semantic
boundary for its questionable negative; W05 needs reconciliation of request scope

count. No such repairs were made in this review.
