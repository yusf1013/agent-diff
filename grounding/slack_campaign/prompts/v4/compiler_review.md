Review one compiled world against its original conceptual sketch and request.
Mechanical seed/query checks have already run. Their success is not a semantic
verdict. API capabilities and setup are supplied domain facts; do not invent
access prerequisites or attempt a separate access proof. No solver outcome is used.

1. Compare request, story selection conditions and structured selector. Identify
   missing/invented conditions, hidden-ID restrictions or changed relationship
   roles. A keyword test need not represent a semantic topic faithfully.
2. Follow each story row through its root/support bindings into the actual seed.
   Check its concrete content, shared records and required passing/failing facts.
   Reconstruct intended matches or alternatives from the request, not the labels.
3. Inspect setup and filler for additional matches, unintended alternatives or
   connections across rows. Check that the card and task specification preserve
   the requested operation and use the correct native attributes.
4. Assess quality separately: credible near misses, ordinary short wording,
   natural content, useful background, no answer hints. An inherited missing
   negative is a quality issue; do not require redesign to make compilation pass.

Return JSON {"validity":"pass|fail|unresolved","quality":"strong|adequate|weak",
"issues":[{"kind":"validity|quality","origin":"design|compilation|selector",
"location":"specific row/field","problem":"concrete evidence",
"repair":"smallest correction or required design revision"}],
"rationale":"brief evidence"}.
Do not rewrite the test. The compiler may repair concrete instantiation errors;
semantic selector or source-design changes require separate review. Describe the
actual defect rather than asserting that checks passed or failed.
