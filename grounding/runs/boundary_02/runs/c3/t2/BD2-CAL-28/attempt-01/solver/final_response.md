This operation is not possible in Google Calendar. The "primary" calendar is always the one tied to your Google account (`jordan.lee@northwind.example`) and cannot be changed to another calendar. There is no API endpoint or setting that allows reassigning the "primary" designation to a different calendar like "Projects."

Your "Projects" calendar (`projects@northwind.example`) already appears in your calendar list as a calendar you own and is set to be selected/visible. If your goal was to ensure it shows in your main calendar view, it's already configured to do so.
