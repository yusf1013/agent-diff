I suspect the validator is merging bindings for both target_user and admin_namesake roles into one obligation set for O1, when really only target_user's binding should count toward the match.

Given the design keeps both roles in target_roles, the validator apparently just aggregates all bindings under those role labels regardless of selection type, ignoring the negative distinction. So the fix is likely to exclude admin_namesake from the bindings dict entirely, since negative_referents can reference the handle directly without needing it registered under target_roles.
